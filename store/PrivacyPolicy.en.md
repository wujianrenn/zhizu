# Privacy Policy — 值物 (Zhizu)

_Last updated: June 9, 2026_

Thank you for using **值物 (Zhizu)** ("the App"). Your privacy matters to us. This
policy explains, plainly and accurately, how the App handles your information.

## Summary

**The App collects no personal data.** Everything you enter stays on your device.
There are no analytics, no advertising, no third‑party SDKs, no tracking, and no
user account.

## What data the App stores

The App lets you record your belongings, costs, statistics, and a wishlist. All of
this content (asset names, prices, dates, categories, notes, wishlist items, theme
and appearance preferences) is stored **locally on your device** using Apple's
**SwiftData** framework. This data never leaves your device through the App and is
never transmitted to us or to any third party.

## Data we do NOT collect

- We do **not** collect your name, email, phone number, or any contact information.
- We do **not** collect location, photos, contacts, or device identifiers.
- We do **not** use analytics or crash‑reporting services.
- We do **not** include advertising or third‑party tracking SDKs.
- We do **not** require an account or sign‑in.

## iCloud sync (optional, future)

If a future version offers iCloud synchronization, it would use **your own private
iCloud account** via Apple's CloudKit. In that case your data is stored in your
personal iCloud container, governed by Apple's privacy terms, and remains
inaccessible to the developer. This is entirely optional and off by default.

## Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone, because we never receive it
in the first place.

## Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

## Your control over your data

Because all data lives on your device, you are in full control. You can edit or
delete any entry at any time, export your data, or delete the App to remove all of
its data from your device.

new "Last updated" date.
